Scaling Mentorship: Earning Top Tutor at Coursehero

Created on May 11, 2023

2023   ·   teaching   mathematics   computer-science   coursehero   ·   Mentorship   Mathematics   Engineering

During my third year at IIT Bombay, I wanted a practical way to apply my passion for mathematics and problem-solving outside of my standard coursework. I decided to apply as a tutor on Coursehero (now part of Learneo) to see if I could effectively teach these concepts to others. What started as an experiment turned into a highly rewarding stint that lasted for almost a full year.

Starting in Electrical Engineering

I initially joined the platform strictly as an Electrical Engineering tutor. My goal was straightforward. I didn’t want to just hand out static answers. I wanted to break down complex, advanced engineering bottlenecks into logical steps that a student sitting anywhere in the world could easily follow.

Remote mentoring is uniquely challenging because you have to diagnose exactly where a student is stuck purely through text and digital equations. I put a lot of effort into making my explanations as clear and fundamentally sound as possible. That practical approach paid off quickly, and I managed to maintain a flawless 100% helpful rate for several consecutive months.

Upgrading and Expanding Domains

Because of that consistent positive response and effort, the Coursehero core team took notice. They directly connected with me and upgraded my profile, unlocking a much wider range of subjects. I went from exclusively handling EE questions to actively tutoring across Mathematics, Electrical Engineering, and Computer Science.

Over the course of the year, I systematically tackled over 200 advanced questions across these three domains. Maintaining high academic integrity and precision was my top priority, and even with the expanded workload, I held onto a 95% overall positive rating from the student community.

The Takeaway: Top Tutor Status and $3,000

The metrics by the end of my run were highly satisfying. I earned almost $3,000 USD over the year and was officially awarded the title of Top Tutor. More importantly, establishing a direct connection with the core team gave me a great inside look at how global EdTech platforms operate, evaluate talent, and scale their mentorship.

Eventually, I reached a point where I felt completely fulfilled with the role and what I had accomplished, so I decided to step away to focus on my next set of engineering challenges. Beyond the financial reward and the official badges, that year fundamentally sharpened my ability to communicate highly technical concepts with extreme clarity. That specific skill has proven to be incredibly useful for my broader career in engineering and software architecture.
